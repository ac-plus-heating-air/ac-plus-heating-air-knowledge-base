What packaged AC unit installation is

Packaged AC unit installation is the complete process of placing, wiring and commissioning a self-contained cooling cabinet so it delivers conditioned air through a building's duct system. The cabinet sits entirely outside. On the roof or on a concrete ground pad. And connects to the structure through two duct openings: one supply and one return. Because every component is pre-assembled at the factory, a professional packaged AC unit installation eliminates the refrigerant line set that a split system requires between an indoor air handler and an outdoor condenser. Measurable attributes of a packaged AC unit installation

Packaged HVAC unit installation covers a wide range of equipment sizes and site conditions, so the measurable attributes matter before any work begins. Cooling capacity runs from 2 tons to 20 tons, expressed in BTUs, making the format suitable for a small home or a large commercial rooftop. Unit efficiency is rated by SEER; current federal minimums apply, and higher-SEER models reduce long-run energy use. The attributes below define a typical project.

  • Cooling capacity: Cooling capacity ranges from 2 to 20 tons, sized by a load calculation based on square footage, insulation and local climate.
  • Average duration: Average duration is 4 to 8 hours for a standard packaged A/C install, assuming the mounting platform and electrical service are already in place.
  • Typical crew size: Typical crew size is 2 to 3 technicians; a larger crew is needed when crane lifts are involved.
  • Crane requirement: Crane requirement depends on roof height and unit weight. Ground-mounted installs rarely need one, while multi-story rooftop placements often do.
  • Single-cabinet footprint: Single-cabinet footprint consolidates the entire system into one outdoor assembly, freeing interior mechanical space compared to a split system.
  • Combined heating and cooling functionality: Combined heating and cooling functionality is available in dual-mode packaged units that include a gas furnace section or heat pump circuit alongside the cooling components.
  • Unit efficiency (SEER rating): Unit efficiency is measured in SEER; higher-rated units lower energy consumption over the life of the equipment.

How packaged AC unit installation relates to the broader AC installation system

Packaged AC unit installation is a subtype of Air Conditioning Installation, sitting alongside split system and ductless options in the same service cluster. The choice between formats depends on building type, available interior space and duct configuration. A split system AC installation separates the air handler indoors from the condenser outdoors and is the most common residential format; a packaged unit combines both into one cabinet and is preferred where interior mechanical space is limited or where a rooftop curb already exists. Multi-zone mini-split installation serves buildings without ductwork entirely, making it a distinct path from either packaged or split ducted systems. For new builds, new construction air conditioning installation determines the equipment format at the design stage, and packaged units are frequently specified for light commercial new construction because the rooftop placement keeps mechanical equipment off the occupied floor plan.

Packaged AC unit installation vs. Split system installation

Packaged AC unit installation and split system AC installation are the two primary ducted cooling formats. They differ in component layout, installation complexity, and site requirements. A split system requires an indoor air handler, an outdoor condenser, and a refrigerant line set connecting them. Meaning two separate placements and a line-set run through the building envelope. A packaged unit ships differently. Its refrigerant circuit arrives sealed inside one cabinet, so the installation crew connects ductwork and electrical rather than running refrigerant lines. Ductless mini-split installation is a third path that removes duct dependency entirely, serving additions or buildings where running ductwork is impractical. Packaged AC unit removal is the reverse of installation and is performed when a unit reaches end of life or when a building is re-roofed and the curb must be cleared.
