### What package unit repair is

Package unit repair is a type of Air Conditioning Repair Service that restores a packaged HVAC cabinet to full heating and cooling operation. A packaged unit differs from a split system in one key structural way. There is no indoor air handler in a separate location. Instead, the refrigerant circuit, blower motor, electrical controls, and heat exchanger all share one outdoor cabinet. That design simplifies installation. It means a single refrigerant leak, failed capacitor, or cracked heat exchanger takes down both heating and cooling simultaneously. Rooftop AC Repair addresses faults in that shared cabinet rather than tracing problems across two separate indoor and outdoor units.

### Common problems that require rooftop unit repair

Rooftop Unit Repair most often addresses five recurring faults in packaged cabinets. The problems are listed below.

- **Refrigerant leaks:** Refrigerant leaks drop system pressure and reduce cooling output; R-410A and R-22 systems both develop leaks at coil joints and service valve fittings over time.

- **Compressor failure:** Compressor failure stops the refrigerant circuit entirely and is the most expensive single repair in a packaged cabinet, often prompting a replacement evaluation.

- **Electrical control board faults:** Electrical control board faults cause erratic cycling, no-start conditions or failure to switch between heating and cooling modes.

- **Heat exchanger cracks:** Heat exchanger cracks in gas-pack units allow combustion gases to enter the airstream, making prompt repair a safety matter rather than a comfort one.

- **Capacitor and contactor wear:** Capacitor and contactor wear is the most frequent cause of a packaged unit that hums but does not start, and replacement typically takes under an hour once diagnosed.
